# Maze image assets

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool for this project. These textures
appear in natural-grass and rooftop-alley landscapes throughout the 200-level
journey. Levels 8 and 9 retain the original sample scenes. The actual maze
geometry defines every barrier and alley.

## natural_grass.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a seamless square ground texture for a mobile top-down maze game. Primary request: recognizable real-looking dense fresh green grass, viewed exactly vertically from above. Full-frame continuous lawn of thin varied overlapping blades, lush but natural green with a few pale yellow-green blades, fine organic detail, even soft daylight, no horizon, no perspective, no objects, no flowers, no borders, no text, no watermark. Texture should tile with visually matching opposite edges, balanced density and illumination throughout, not a central tuft or illustration. The texture will be shown in narrow strips as living grass barriers in a game, so retain clear natural blade structure and medium green tonal contrast. 1024 by 1024 square.

## city_rooftop.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one square top-down rooftop game tile for a mobile maze city, inspired by recognizable low-rise Dhaka neighborhood buildings. Primary request: a single detailed believable square flat-roof concrete residential building seen from directly overhead in orthographic plan, filling the entire square image edge to edge with its roof and perimeter parapet. Warm cream weathered concrete roof, thin terracotta parapet all four edges, small rooftop stairwell room in one corner, a blue cylindrical water tank, a few rectangular dark blue solar panels, pale mint accents and subtle concrete stains. Restrained natural materials with clear readable architectural shapes, polished realistic miniature game art, soft ambient daylight. Roof and parapet must be aligned horizontally and vertically; no isometric perspective, no visible side facades, no ground or roads around the building, no people, no writing, no labels, no logos, no watermark, no cast shadow outside the square. All details inside the tile, roof fills the complete square with no margins. Designed to look like a real building roof at small scale. 1024 by 1024 square.
